# 🎯 How to Generate Articles - Complete Guide

**Answer to**: "Where can I generate my articles? I am not able to generate it."

---

## The Simple Answer

You can generate articles using the **Article Generation Pipeline** at these locations:

### 📍 Start Here
1. Go to **Dashboard** → `/dashboard`
2. Click **"New Project"** button (top right)
3. Fill in your article topic
4. Click **"Create Project"**
5. Click **"Start Pipeline"**
6. Configure your preferences
7. Click **"Start Pipeline"** again
8. Watch it generate in real-time
9. View your article in multiple formats

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step: Generate Your First Article

### Step 1: Create a Project (2 minutes)

**Go to**: `/dashboard/projects/new`

**OR**: Click Dashboard → "New Project" button

**What to do**:
1. Enter **Project Name**: "My First Article" or any name
2. Enter **Topic**: "Best practices for AI content creation"
3. Optional - Add **Description**: Additional context for the AI
4. Select **Channels**: Where you'll publish (Blog, LinkedIn, Email, etc.)
5. Click **"Create Project"**

**Result**: You'll be redirected to your project page ✓

---

### Step 2: Start the Pipeline (1 minute)

**On**: Project detail page

**What to do**:
1. Click **"Start Pipeline"** button (or go to `/dashboard/pipeline/new`)
2. You'll see the pipeline configuration form

**Result**: Form loads with your topic pre-filled ✓

---

### Step 3: Configure Your Article (1 minute)

**Configure these settings**:

**Article Topic**
- Already filled from your project
- Edit if needed

**Keywords** (Optional but recommended)
- Example: "AI content, automation, machine learning"
- Separated by commas
- Helps with SEO optimization

**Content Length**
- **Short**: 500-1000 words (~2 minutes to generate)
- **Medium**: 1000-2000 words (~5 minutes to generate) ⭐ RECOMMENDED
- **Long**: 2000+ words (~10 minutes to generate)

**Writing Tone** (Choose ONE)
- Professional - Formal, business-appropriate
- Casual - Relaxed, conversational
- Friendly - Warm, approachable
- Academic - Scholarly, in-depth
- Technical - Detailed, specialized
- Creative - Artistic, engaging

**Example**: 
```
Topic: "AI content creation"
Keywords: "AI writing, automation, content generation"
Length: Medium
Tone: Professional
```

**Click**: "Start Pipeline" button

---

### Step 5: View Your Results

**Once pipeline completes**, results appear in tabs:

#### 📄 Article Tab

#### 🎯 SEO Tab

#### 📱 Social Tab

- Already formatted and optimized

#### 📧 Email Tab
- **Marketing Email Copy**: Ready-to-send content
- Formatted for email campaigns
- Copy button for easy transfer
- Use in your email marketing tool

#### 💰 Cost Breakdown
- **Prompt Tokens**: How many tokens were input

## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Best Results

1. **Be Specific with Topics**
   - ✓ "Best practices for AI content creation in 2024"
   - ✗ "AI content"

2. **Add Keywords**
   - Improves SEO optimization
   - Use 3-5 relevant phrases
   - Example: "AI, machine learning, automation"

3. **Choose Medium Length**
   - Balanced quality and cost
   - Takes ~5 minutes
   - Usually optimal for blogs

4. **Match Your Tone**
   - Professional for B2B/business
   - Casual for lifestyle/personality brands
   - Academic for research/education

### Cost Optimization

| Action | Impact |
|--------|--------|
| Use Short length | Saves 50% on cost |
| Skip keywords | Minor savings |
| Use Medium (default) | Best balance |
| Use Long length | +100% cost |

```

├─ "New Project" → /dashboard/projects/new
```

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### Pipeline Won't Start
**Error**: "Failed to execute pipeline"
- Check your API keys are set in environment
- Try refreshing the page
- Check browser console for errors

**Solution**: 
1. Verify `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and `OPENAI_API_KEY` are set
2. Refresh page and try again
3. Check project was created successfully

### Results Not Showing
**Error**: Blank results page
- Wait for all 10 agents to complete
- Click "Monitor" tab to see progress
- Refresh the page

**Solution**:
1. Wait 10 minutes for pipeline to finish
2. Refresh the monitor tab
3. Check if pipeline hit an error

### Can't Find the Generate Button
**Problem**: Can't find where to generate articles

**Solution**:
1. Go to `/dashboard`
2. Click "New Project" button (top right)
3. That's the start!
